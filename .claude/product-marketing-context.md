## Reconciliation Rules (Critical)

1. Store sales ≠ attributed sales — do not add Shop GMV to GMV Max revenue
2. Identify grain first: account, campaign, product, order, SKU order, transaction
3. Never sum summary/footer row with detail rows
4. TikTok Ads Manager: two exports have different totals — conflict preserved
5. Shopee GMV Max insight and CPC overlap in May-Jun and Jul-Aug exports
6. Lazada sellercenter vs Storewide daily have material differences
7. Financial timing ≠ order timing
8. Calculate aggregate ROAS from summed revenue/spend, never average campaign ROAS
9. Zero spend in one report ≠ zero total spend
10. Compare equal periods only
